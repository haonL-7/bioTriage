PHENOMS: Open-Source Software for Rust-Accelerated Hydrogen-Bond Analysis in Molecular Dynamics Simulations.

PHENOMS is an open-source Python package that combines molecular dynamics trajectories with hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry (HDX-MS) data to analyze backbone hydrogen-bond networks at residue-level resolution. It tracks how individual hydrogen bonds and residue contacts form, break, and reorganize over simulation time, revealing local structural changes that may be missed by other methods. The tool uses Rust-accelerated computation for efficiency.
