This paper proposes Protein eXplosion Imaging (PXI), a method that infers low-resolution protein structure from the ion trajectories produced when proteins are exploded by strong laser ionization. The work suggests that explosion ion distributions retain usable structural information, offering a potential alternative route to low-resolution protein shape determination.
