The authors characterized the microstructure and composition of the recovered fractions and examined how pH affects the physical stability of natural oil bodies, homogenized oil bodies, proteins, and protein-stabilized oil droplets in emulsions. The work supports using these co-extracted rapeseed ingredients as functional emulsifiers in plant-based food formulations.
