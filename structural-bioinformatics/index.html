Infection-osteogenesis crosstalk on TiO2 nanotube surfaces: mechanisms and synergistic design strategies.

This review examines how titanium dioxide nanotube (TNT) surfaces, made by anodization, can be engineered to address both bacterial infection and poor bone integration around orthopedic and dental implants. It argues that infection and bone formation are not separate problems but are linked through shared signaling pathways, and it surveys design strategies that exploit this crosstalk to create implants with combined antibacterial and osteogenic properties.
