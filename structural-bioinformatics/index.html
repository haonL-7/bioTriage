This review discusses structure-based engineering strategies for developing therapeutics targeting PTPσ, a receptor-type protein tyrosine phosphatase involved in neural injury and rheumatoid arthritis. Unique features of EIGHT fold-Proteins with the most complex 83 knotted topology.

This paper reports the first crystal structures of a protein with an 8-crossing knot (8₂₁), the most complex protein knot known to date, found in a specific group of bacteria and strictly conserved despite low sequence identity. The authors define a new fold, the EIGHT fold, which shows internal structural symmetry between halves, suggesting a possible duplication or folding pathway. These structures expand the known limits of protein knot complexity and provide a basis for understanding how such intricate topologies fold and evolve.
