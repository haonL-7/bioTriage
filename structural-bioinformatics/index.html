What deep learning can and cannot (yet) do for enzyme design.

This review examines the current capabilities and limitations of deep learning for enzyme design, highlighting that while diffusion-based scaffold generation combined with sequence design has expanded accessible reaction space and produced enzymes with diverse catalytic functions, reliably achieving high catalytic efficiency remains challenging. Notably, while catalytic efficiency (kcat/KM) can be impressive, turnover rates (kcat) often remain limited, indicating that current methods typically yield tight substrate binding but not optimal catalysis.
