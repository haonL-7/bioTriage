Investigating Ferritin Permeability to Metal-Based Drugs through Molecular Dynamics.

This study used molecular dynamics simulations to test whether gold-based drug complexes can pass through the C3 channels of human H ferritin, a protein cage that could serve as a drug delivery vehicle. The work provides a computational workflow for assessing ferritin permeability to metal-based therapeutics.
