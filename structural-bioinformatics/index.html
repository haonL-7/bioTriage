This study tracked kinase phosphorylation dynamics in RAW264.7 macrophages after SARS-CoV-2 SPIKE transfection and identified p70S6K (RPS6KB1) as a host kinase showing a 3.5-fold increase in activity. The authors propose p70S6K as a potential anti-COVID-19 target involved in the cytokine storm, supported by both wet-lab and in silico analyses.
