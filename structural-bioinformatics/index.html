Impaired IFN-γ/STAT1-associated immune regulation contributes to 4-nonylphenol-aggravated allergic rhinitis.

The findings suggest a mechanistic link between environmental chemical exposure and immune dysregulation in allergic disease.

Molecular Dynamics Simulations of Graphene and Graphene-Derived Ionic Liquid Supercapacitors: A Review of Methods and Applications.

This review surveys molecular dynamics simulation methods for modeling graphene and graphene-derived ionic liquid supercapacitors, focusing on electrode-charging approaches such as fixed-charge, constant-potential, and electronic-structure-aware schemes. Strong light-matter coupling as a photonic substituent: cavity-induced electronic and structural control of regioselectivity in nitrobenzene.

This paper revisits a theoretical prediction that strong light-matter coupling in optical cavities can invert the intrinsic regioselectivity of electrophilic aromatic substitution in nitrobenzene. Using correlated ab initio cavity QED methods (QED-DFT geometry relaxations plus QED-CCSD(2,2) calculations on Wheland intermediates), the authors confirm substantial energetic reordering of the reaction pathways. The work supports the concept of a 'photonic substituent effect' whereby cavity vacuum fields can control ground-state chemical reactivity and selectivity.

Handshake: Partner-specific protein-protein binding site prediction at scale using ProstT5 and cross-chain attention.

HandShake is a sequence-only computational tool that predicts which residues of a protein form the binding interface with a specific partner, using the ProstT5 protein language model combined with cross-chain attention. It addresses limitations of prior methods by training on a larger dataset with consistent redundancy filtering and requiring no 3D structural information at test time. This enables scalable, partner-specific interface prediction with implications for drug discovery and protein function analysis.
