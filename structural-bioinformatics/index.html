Tailoring plant-based food emulsions with oil bodies and proteins recovered by alkali aqueous extraction from rapeseed (Brassica napus L.) oilseeds.

This study demonstrates that oil bodies and storage proteins can be co-extracted from rapeseed using alkaline aqueous extraction at pH 9.5, yielding well-dispersed oil bodies suitable for plant-based food emulsions. The authors characterize the microstructure and composition of the extracts and evaluate how pH affects the physical stability of natural oil bodies, homogenized oil bodies, proteins, and protein-stabilized oil droplets in emulsions. The work provides practical insight into using these co-extracted ingredients to tailor emulsion stability for plant-based food applications.
