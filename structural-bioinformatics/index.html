MHC restriction is structurally encoded by the T cell receptor germline.

This study provides direct structural evidence that T cell receptors (TCRs) recognize MHC molecules through germline-encoded features, independent of the specific peptide antigen. By engineering 'germline-like' TCRs and using a molecular clamping strategy to stabilize ultra-low-affinity complexes, the authors captured cryo-EM structures showing that germline-like TCR/pMHC docking modes are identical to those of wild-type TCRs. This supports the long-standing hypothesis that MHC restriction is hardwired into the TCR germline.
