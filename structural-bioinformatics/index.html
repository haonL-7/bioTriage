Free-Energy and Molecular Dynamics Analysis of Ser195 and Thr195 Rotameric States in Thrombin.

This study uses well-tempered metadynamics and microsecond-scale molecular dynamics simulations to compare the conformational free-energy landscapes of Ser195 and Thr195 in thrombin, addressing why serine is strongly preferred over threonine at this catalytic position in trypsin-like serine proteases. The simulations reveal distinct rotameric preferences between the two residues in both apo and ligand-bound states, with S195 favoring a lower-angle rotamer in the apo state. These findings offer a molecular-level explanation for the evolutionary conservation of serine at the catalytic nucleophile position.
