A water-pinning hot spot drives templated tau aggregation.

This study identifies a single dominant 'water-pinning' hot spot that allows intrinsically disordered tau to stack in register and form seed-competent fibrils capable of templated aggregation. The lysis mechanism of the haloarchaeal virus HFTV1.

This study identifies and characterizes GP33, a small membrane protein that mediates lysis of the archaeal host Haloferax gibbonsii by the tailed archaeal virus HFTV1. Structure-based discovery of a novel Granzyme A (GZMA) inhibitor for autism spectrum disorder through integrative transcriptomics, virtual screening, and long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations.

The authors reanalyzed a peripheral blood transcriptome dataset from autism spectrum disorder (ASD) patients and identified Granzyme A (GZMA), a serine protease that triggers pyroptosis via gasdermin B cleavage, as a potential drug target linked to immune dysregulation. They then used structure-based virtual screening and long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations to discover a novel small-molecule GZMA inhibitor. The work is entirely computational, with no experimental validation of the compound's activity or efficacy.
