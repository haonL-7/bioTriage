CryoID2: deep learning-enhanced identification of protein and RNA from cryoEM maps.

CryoID2 is a new computational tool that uses deep learning to identify proteins and RNA within cryoEM and cryoET density maps of unknown or poorly characterized complexes. Three 3D U-Nets predict structural features such as key-atom positions and reduced residue types, which guide backbone tracing and generate short sequence queries that are matched against user-supplied sequence pools.
