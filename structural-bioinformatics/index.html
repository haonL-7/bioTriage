Thermostable Carbohydrate-Active Enzymes for High-Temperature Biomass Conversion: From Molecular Design to Process Integration.

This review examines the molecular and structural basis of thermostability in carbohydrate-active enzymes (CAZymes) used for high-temperature biomass conversion, such as starch liquefaction and lignocellulose saccharification at 60-90 °C. The work aims to bridge multiscale molecular mechanisms with engineering strategies rarely integrated in prior reviews.

Using virtual screening and long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations, they discovered a novel small-molecule inhibitor of GZMA. This work proposes a structure-based drug discovery strategy for a subset of ASD cases, though experimental validation is still needed.
