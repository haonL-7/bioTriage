What deep learning can and cannot (yet) do for enzyme design.

This review examines the current capabilities and limitations of deep learning for enzyme design, highlighting that while diffusion-based scaffold generation combined with sequence design has expanded accessible reaction space and produced enzymes with diverse catalytic functions, reliably achieving high catalytic efficiency remains elusive. The key bottleneck is that while catalytic efficiencies (kcat/KM) can be notable, turnover rates (kcat) often remain limited, suggesting current approaches typically yield tight substrate binding but poor product release.
