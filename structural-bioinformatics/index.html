This study uses all-atom molecular dynamics simulations to evaluate newly designed ciprofloxacin derivatives as potential inhibitors of two bacterial targets: dihydropteroate synthase (DHPS) and DNA gyrase. The work aims to identify compounds that could overcome multidrug resistance in uropathogenic E. coli by binding these enzymes, providing a computational basis for further antibiotic development.
