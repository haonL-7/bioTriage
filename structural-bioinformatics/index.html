Structure-based minimization of a high-mobility group protein B1 (HMGB1) B box peptide uncouples anti-NETotic activity and anti-biofilm function.

The authors truncated the HMGB1 B-box-derived peptide mB Box-97 into shorter variants containing Helix 1 (with the C106S mutation) to test whether its anti-biofilm and anti-NETosis activities could be separated. They found that these functions can be uncoupled into distinct shorter peptides, suggesting the two activities are structurally separable.
