Free-Energy and Molecular Dynamics Analysis of Ser195 and Thr195 Rotameric States in Thrombin.

This study uses well-tempered metadynamics and microsecond molecular dynamics simulations to compare the conformational energetics of serine versus threonine at position 195 in thrombin, a site where serine is strongly conserved across trypsin-like serine proteases. The authors find that S195 and T195 occupy distinct rotameric free-energy landscapes in both apo and ligand-bound states, with S195 favoring a lower-angle rotamer in the apo state. The work offers a molecular explanation for why serine is preferred over the chemically similar threonine at the catalytic nucleophile position.

Edge-Aware Graph Attention Networks for Interpreting Biophysical Mechanisms from Molecular Dynamics Simulations.

This preprint introduces three edge-aware graph attention network variants (Edge-Conditioned, Edge-Injected, Edge-Gated) for learning biomolecular dynamics from molecular dynamics simulations, differing in how edge features are incorporated during message passing. The authors show these models improve prediction relative to a standard GAT and, importantly, use the learned attention weights to derive 'communication networks' that offer mechanistic interpretation of biophysical processes. The work aims to bridge predictive AI with interpretable mechanistic insight from MD data.

The authors show that AlphaFold2's predicted residue-residue distance distributions (distograms) contain information about multiple protein conformations, even when a single structure is output.
