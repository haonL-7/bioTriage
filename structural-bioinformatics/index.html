Exploring Non-Covalent Pin1-PROTACs Insights Into Degradation Efficiency in Multiple Myeloma Cell Line.

This study explores non-covalent PROTACs targeting the Pin1 isomerase, which is upregulated in multiple myeloma and linked to poor prognosis. Tailoring plant-based food emulsions with oil bodies and proteins recovered by alkali aqueous extraction from rapeseed (Brassica napus L.) oilseeds.

This study demonstrates that oil bodies and storage proteins can be co-extracted from rapeseed using alkaline aqueous extraction at pH 9.5, yielding well-dispersed oil bodies suitable for plant-based food emulsions. The authors characterize the microstructure and composition of the recovered fractions and examine how pH affects the physical stability of natural oil bodies, homogenized oil bodies, proteins, and protein-stabilized oil droplets in emulsions.

Exploring molecular interactions between yeast topoisomerase I and RNA polymerase III subunit, Rpc82 using an in-silico approach.

This study uses computational docking and molecular dynamics simulations to model how yeast topoisomerase I (Top I) interacts with Rpc82, a subunit of RNA polymerase III. The authors identify putative interaction interfaces and structural domains that may mediate this binding, offering hypotheses about how Top I is recruited to relieve transcription-associated torsional stress. The work is purely in-silico and provides a structural framework for future experimental validation.

Sequence-activity mapping and model-guided engineering of the phenol-responsive DmpR-Po σ54 promoter.

This study systematically maps how sequence variation across the regulatory regions of the phenol-responsive DmpR-Po σ54 promoter affects transcriptional output, then uses the resulting data to build a model that guides engineering of improved promoter variants. It shows that the combined contributions of distal enhancer-binding sites, DNA architectural elements, and core promoter sequences can be quantitatively predicted and optimized, enabling better-tuned whole-cell biosensors.
