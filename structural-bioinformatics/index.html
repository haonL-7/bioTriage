They found that anti-NETotic and anti-biofilm functions can be uncoupled into distinct peptide regions, enabling separation of these activities. This structure-guided minimization could support development of peptide therapeutics with reduced off-target inflammatory effects.

Unleashing the industrial potential of microbial plant cell wall polysaccharide-degrading enzymes: recent advances in protein engineering.

This review surveys recent protein engineering advances aimed at improving microbial plant cell wall polysaccharide-degrading enzymes (cellulases, LPMOs, xylanases, mannanases, pectinases) for industrial use. It focuses on how structure- and sequence-guided engineering strategies address limitations such as low catalytic efficiency, poor thermostability, restricted operational adaptability, and product inhibition across biorefinery, food/feed, textile, and pulp applications.
