## 1. [High] Small molecule structure elucidation from NMR spectra with generative and reasoning models.

- 频道：ai-drug-discovery
- 期刊：bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology ｜ 2026-10-31
- DOI：10.64898/2026.10.29.762711
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：The authors present an AI system that combines a molecular generative model (CHEFNMR2, an atomic flow-matching model) with a scientific reasoning agent to automatically determine the structure of unknown small molecules from NMR spectra. This addresses the labor-intensive, expert-dependent bottleneck in NMR-based structure elucidation, which is central to chemistry, pharmacology, and drug discovery.

## 2. [High] Reference-free isoform discovery links unannotated RNA processing to disease risk.

- 频道：computational-genomics
- 期刊：bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology ｜ 2026-10-23
- DOI：10.64898/2026.10.21.760529
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：The authors present Torino, a computational framework that discovers RNA processing patterns directly from population-scale RNA sequencing data without relying on existing transcript annotations. By adapting non-negative matrix factorization to model how read coverage varies across samples, Torino identifies unannotated RNA processing events and quantifies their usage across tissues, individuals, and disease states, revealing links between previously hidden processing patterns and disease risk.

## 3. [High] InterPro: the protein sequence classification resource in 2027.

- 频道：databases-knowledge-graphs
- 期刊：Nucleic acids research ｜ 2026-11-02
- DOI：10.1093/nar/gkag1114
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：InterPro is a freely accessible database that integrates predictive models (signatures) from multiple member databases to classify protein sequences into families and predict domains and functional sites. The 2027 update provides annotations for hundreds of millions of sequences, achieving extensive coverage of UniProtKB and linking to resources like Gene Ontology and PDBe. This makes it a central hub for protein functional annotation.

## 4. [Medium] Gut‒liver‒brain axis signature of cirrhosis-related cognitive impairment is consistent across multi-national cohorts.

- 频道：metagenomics-informatics
- 期刊：Gut microbes ｜ 2026-12-31
- DOI：10.1080/19490976.2026.2753908
- bioTriage 分级：**Medium**
- bioTriage 摘要：This study examined whether gut microbiome signatures associated with cognitive impairment in cirrhosis are consistent across patients from four countries (USA, Denmark, Lithuania, Mexico). Using stool shotgun metagenomics and PHES testing for covert hepatic encephalopathy, the authors identified taxonomic and functional microbial associations with cognitive scores that held across diverse geographic cohorts despite differences in diet and clinical practice. The findings support a reproducible gut‒liver‒brain axis signature underlying cirrhosis-related cognitive impairment.

## 5. [High] Single-cell analysis of chromatin state and transcriptome in the human basal ganglia.

- 频道：ml-biology
- 期刊：Cell ｜ 2026-11-12
- DOI：10.1016/j.cell.2026.09.008
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：This study presents a single-cell multi-omic atlas of histone modifications and transcriptomes across eight human basal ganglia regions, revealing cell-type- and region-specific regulatory landscapes and gene regulatory networks. The authors identify combinatorial homeobox transcription factor expression as a key driver of cell identity in this brain region. This resource provides a foundation for understanding basal ganglia function and dysfunction in neurological and psychiatric disorders.

## 6. [High] A droplet-based long-read single-cell epigenome sequencing method for the high-throughput profiling of multidimensional regulatory elements within individual cells.

- 频道：single-cell-omics
- 期刊：Cell discovery ｜ 2026-10-27
- DOI：10.1038/s41421-026-00928-y
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：The authors developed droplet-based long-read single-cell epigenome sequencing methods, dscNanoATAC and dscNanoCUT, by combining a commercial microfluidic device with nanopore sequencing to profile chromatin accessibility and histone modifications in individual cells at high throughput. This addresses previous scalability limitations of long-read single-cell epigenomics, enabling multidimensional regulatory element profiling within single cells.

## 7. [Medium] Beyond AlphaFold 3: a standardised benchmark of open-source co-folding models for antibody-antigen complexes, from docking accuracy to interaction specificity.

- 频道：structural-bioinformatics
- 期刊：mAbs ｜ 2026-12-31
- DOI：10.1080/19420862.2026.2760737
- bioTriage 分级：**Medium**
- bioTriage 摘要：This paper benchmarks five open-source co-folding models (AlphaFold 3, Protenix-v2, ESMFold2, ESMFold2-fast, and OpenDDE) on antibody-antigen complex prediction using a newly curated dataset of 200 complexes (SAbDab2-Bench) plus a reconstructed FoldBench antibody subset. It evaluates docking accuracy and interaction specificity under standardized inputs, sampling, and interface metrics, providing a fair comparison of the latest generation of co-folding tools for antibody-antigen modeling.

## 8. [High] PIANO enables generative modeling of gene expression and scales multi-omics integration to 100 million cells.

- 频道：systems-biology
- 期刊：Cell reports methods ｜ 2026-11-12
- DOI：10.1016/j.crmeth.2026.101661
- bioTriage 分级：**High**
- bioTriage 摘要：PIANO is a deep learning framework that uses a generative model of gene expression to integrate single-cell multi-omics data. It addresses three key challenges in data integration: correcting strong batch effects, scaling to millions of cells (demonstrated up to 100 million), and modeling how covariates influence gene expression. This enables more accurate comparisons across large-scale transcriptomic atlases.
