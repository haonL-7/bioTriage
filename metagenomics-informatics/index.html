Multi-scale hybrid correction of noisy long reads.

DADEC is a new hybrid error-correction method for noisy long sequencing reads that combines stringent de Bruijn graph correction, haplotype-aware short-read alignment refinement, and permissive de Bruijn graph recovery in a confidence-ordered pipeline. Across seven simulated and three real datasets, it achieved the lowest mismatch and indel rates in most benchmarks while avoiding over-correction that erases genuine biological variation. The approach aims to balance error suppression with preservation of true sequence diversity.
