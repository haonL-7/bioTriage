Genome-resolved ecology and evolution of the microbiome enabled by quantitative long-read metagenomics.

The authors present long-read quantiomics (LRQ), a metagenomics platform that combines long-read sequencing with plasmid-based internal standards to recover high-quality genomes and measure absolute, genome-resolved strain abundances, even from degraded or low-input stool samples. They validate the method with plasmid standards and bacterial spike-ins, then apply it to eight years of dense longitudinal sampling to track how individual strains change in absolute abundance and evolve within a host. This enables genome-resolved ecological and evolutionary analysis of the microbiome that relative-abundance methods cannot provide.
