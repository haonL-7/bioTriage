Scellmate enables contamination aware curation and host mobilome linkage analysis of large SAG datasets.

Scaffold-level validation is needed for phage-host inference from metagenome-assembled genomes.

The paper argues that phage-host predictions based on metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) can be unreliable because MAG-level quality metrics like completeness and contamination do not guarantee scaffold-level correctness. Using tetranucleotide frequency analysis, the authors show that CRISPR-associated regions often differ compositionally from their host genomes, increasing the risk of misassigning phage-host relationships. They call for scaffold-level validation in phage-host inference workflows.

This study used shotgun metagenomics to profile the fecal microbiome of 55 physicians caring for cirrhosis patients across 9 countries, examining carriage of antimicrobial resistance genes (ARGs) and virulence factors (VFs).
