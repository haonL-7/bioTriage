By mining over 40 billion predicted open reading frames from tens of thousands of metagenomes and metatranscriptomes, the authors identified 608,258 previously uncharacterized protein families with at least 100 members, plus 6.5 million smaller families, none of which match known Pfam domains or reference proteins.
