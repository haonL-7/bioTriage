From saccharides to methane: substrate-driven microbial diversity and methanogenesis in anaerobic digestion revealed by 390 MAGs.

This study reconstructed 390 high-quality metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) from synthetic anaerobic digestion systems fed with five different substrates, revealing that substrate type strongly selects for distinct microbial consortia. Nearly two-thirds of the MAGs represent potentially novel species, and specific taxa like Bifidobacterium were enriched for starch hydrolysis while syntrophic bacteria dominated other substrate conditions. The work provides a genome-resolved view of how substrate chemistry drives microbial community structure and methanogenesis in anaerobic digestion.
