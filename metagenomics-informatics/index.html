Multi-scale hybrid correction of noisy long reads.

DADEC is a new hybrid error-correction method for noisy long sequencing reads that uses a three-stage confidence-ordered pipeline: stringent de Bruijn graph correction, haplotype-aware short-read alignment refinement, and permissive de Bruijn graph recovery. Across seven simulated and three real datasets, it achieved the lowest mismatch and indel rates in most benchmarks while avoiding over-correction of genuine biological variation. The approach targets the key trade-off between suppressing sequencing errors and preserving true haplotype diversity.
