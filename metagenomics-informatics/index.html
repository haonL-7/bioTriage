Evaluating traditional Chinese medicine modulation of the gut microbiota-bile acid axis in metabolic and inflammatory diseases: mechanistic evidence and translational priorities.

This review examines how traditional Chinese medicine may influence metabolic and inflammatory diseases (MASLD, obesity, T2D, IBD) through the gut microbiota–bile acid axis. The authors argue that many reported microbial shifts are over-interpreted as mechanisms without functional or bile-acid evidence, and they outline translational priorities to strengthen causal inference. It is a critical synthesis rather than new primary data.
