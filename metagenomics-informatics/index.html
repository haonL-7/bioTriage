iScience 2026-11-20 Medium

Multi-scale hybrid correction of noisy long reads.

DADEC is a new hybrid error-correction method for noisy long reads that uses a three-stage confidence-ordered pipeline: stringent de Bruijn graph correction, haplotype-aware short-read alignment refinement, and permissive de Bruijn graph recovery. Across seven simulated and three real datasets, it achieved the lowest mismatch and indel rates in most benchmarks while preserving genuine biological variation, avoiding the over-correction problem of existing tools.

Draft genome sequences of Desulfobulbus oralis strains reconstructed from archeological dental calculus of ancient Pacific Islanders.

Researchers reconstructed draft genomes of Desulfobulbus oralis, an oral bacterium, from ancient dental calculus (calcified plaque) of Pacific Islanders using metagenomic assembly and binning. The genomes are highly similar (≥96.4% ANI) to a modern reference strain but are only moderately complete (≥50%). This demonstrates that oral microbiome DNA can be recovered from archaeological dental calculus, though the genomes are too incomplete for detailed functional analysis.

A mosaic of bacterial communities and their functional potential across Antarctic moss, lichen and soil.
