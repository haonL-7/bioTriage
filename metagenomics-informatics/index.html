Obtaining maximum information from colony counts with REPOP.

REPOP is a new statistical method that corrects for a common bias in estimating bacterial population sizes from dilution plating experiments. Standard approaches multiply colony counts by dilution factors, which artificially inflates apparent heterogeneity by mixing true biological variability with sampling noise. REPOP separates these sources of variation, yielding more accurate estimates of bacterial population sizes and their true variability in native environments like soil or gut.
