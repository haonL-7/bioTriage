iScience 2026-11-20 Medium

Multi-scale hybrid correction of noisy long reads.

DADEC is a new hybrid error-correction method for noisy long reads that uses a confidence-ordered three-stage framework: stringent de Bruijn graph correction, haplotype-aware short-read alignment refinement, and permissive de Bruijn graph recovery. Across seven simulated and three real datasets, it achieved the lowest mismatch and indel rates in most benchmarks while avoiding over-correction of genuine biological variation.

Aerobic methane oxidation by microorganisms exacerbates neurotoxic methylmercury accumulation in rice paddy fields.

A large-scale field survey across China's rice-growing regions, combined with 13CH4-DNA stable isotope probing, shows that aerobic methane-oxidizing microorganisms are linked to increased microbial mercury methylation in paddy soils.
