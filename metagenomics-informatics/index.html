Short-read genome skimming enables molecular barcoding of old myxomycete collections.

This study demonstrates that Illumina genome skimming can successfully recover barcode and additional marker sequences from myxomycete herbarium specimens up to 91 years old, with partial nucSSU recovered in all cases.
