The authors present GDA-Pred, a framework that uses generative AI to create synthetic peptide data and thereby augment the limited experimentally validated datasets for IL-6- and IL-13-inducing peptides. This data augmentation improves the performance of machine learning/deep learning predictors for identifying immunomodulatory peptides relevant to cancer, immune disorders, and infectious disease drug discovery. The work addresses the common bottleneck of scarce labeled data in peptide bioactivity prediction.
