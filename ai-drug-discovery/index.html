DrugMAP 3.0: an enriched molecular atlas featuring ADMET endpoints, pharmacogenomic perturbations, and bioactivity atlas of drugs.

DrugMAP 3.0 is a substantially expanded database that integrates three previously fragmented dimensions of drug pharmacology: ADMET (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity) endpoints, transcriptomic perturbation signatures, and quantitative drug-target binding affinities. By consolidating systemic safety profiles, cellular responses, and molecular interactions into a single multidimensional atlas, it addresses a gap in existing drug databases that typically cover only one of these layers. The resource is intended to support drug repurposing, safety assessment, and mechanistic studies of drug action.
