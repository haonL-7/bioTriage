This study uses multi-scale computational docking and simulation approaches to screen phytochemicals from the mangrove plant Rhizophora mucronata as potential inhibitors of lung cancer-associated protein targets. The authors identify candidate compounds with favorable binding and stability profiles, proposing them as starting points for novel low-toxicity NSCLC therapeutics. The work is entirely in silico, so experimental validation of the predicted anticancer activity remains to be done.
