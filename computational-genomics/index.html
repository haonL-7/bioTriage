DUET-seq is an open-source droplet microfluidic platform that jointly profiles chromatin accessibility and gene expression from the same nucleus, using programmable dissolvable dual-linker hydrogel beads and one-step intra-droplet RT-PCR to co-index RNA and transposed chromatin fragments.
