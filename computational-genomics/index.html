Gravlax: an annotation-independent molecular evidence archive for single-cell RNA-seq.

Gravlax introduces an annotation-independent archive that stores molecular evidence from single-cell RNA-seq experiments in a compact form, rather than relying on the standard cell-by-gene count matrix. Because gene annotations are continually revised, the count matrix locks in an outdated interpretation; Gravlax preserves the underlying molecule-level evidence so it can be reinterpreted later without returning to raw reads or alignments. This enables more durable and flexible reanalysis of scRNA-seq data.
