Species phylogenetics using intraprotein residue dependence inferred with protein foundation models.

This preprint proposes using protein foundation models (pFMs) to infer phylogenetic relationships by capturing intraprotein residue dependencies, rather than relying on traditional alignment-based substitution models that assume site independence. De novo Design of a Vanadium Haloperoxidase.

This preprint reports the de novo design of a protein that binds vanadate and functions as a vanadium-dependent haloperoxidase, an enzyme class that catalyzes carbon-halogen bond formation. The key challenge was that vanadate is an anionic oxometalate requiring a precise hydrogen-bonding and electrostatic network rather than the coordinate-covalent bonds used to bind typical cationic metals.
