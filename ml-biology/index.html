Protein sequence domain annotation using a language model.

PSALM is a new method for annotating protein domains that combines a pretrained protein language model (ESM-2) with a per-residue domain-state classifier and a structured probabilistic decoder to produce non-overlapping domain calls with explicit boundaries and scores. Benchmarked on 76.9M protein sequences with 107M annotated domains, it improves domain-detection sensitivity over traditional profile HMM scanning. This offers a language-model-based alternative to the standard HMM-based annotation pipeline.
