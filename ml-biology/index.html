Germline dependence in protein language model retrieval of adaptive immune receptors.

This study benchmarks protein language models (PLMs) against classical sequence-comparison methods for retrieving antigen-specific B- and T-cell receptors across four immune receptor databases. Using CDR3 sequences alone, classical methods like BLOSUM62 and Levenshtein consistently outperformed general PLMs (e.g., ESM2) by 7-12 percentage points in retrieval accuracy, with only receptor-specific models such as SCEPTR showing competitive performance on one database. The findings suggest that general PLMs may not offer an advantage over simple sequence comparison for this task, highlighting germline dependence in receptor representation.
