Cellular scaling laws in the mammalian brain.

The paper proposes that whole-brain cell atlases reveal a fundamental division in the vertebrate brain between a "learning subsystem" (cortex, striatum, cerebellum) that scales by replicating repetitive modules for computational capacity, and a "steering subsystem" (hypothalamus, brain stem) that harbors the greatest diversity of discrete neuronal cell types.
