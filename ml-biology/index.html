A spectral framework for measuring diversity in multiple sequence alignments.

The authors introduce Leff, a spectral measure that quantifies how many effectively independent positions exist in a multiple sequence alignment, capturing the true usable information content. Applied to RNA alignments, it reveals that evolutionary constraints cut sequence diversity nearly in half compared to what secondary structure alone would predict. This provides a principled way to assess whether MSAs contain enough information for machine learning models.
