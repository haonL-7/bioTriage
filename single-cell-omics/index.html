Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

This paper proposes a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, using a gradient-boosted meta-classifier trained on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe). Instead of choosing one method or averaging all methods, the classifier learns to weight each method differently for each candidate regulator-target edge, and the authors also develop a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflicts. The approach is evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens.
