Denoising single-cell ATAC-seq data with Shannon entropy.

The authors introduce a Shannon entropy-based method for filtering low-quality nuclei from single-cell ATAC-seq data, addressing the problem of empty or damaged droplets that introduce noise and obscure rare cell populations. Unlike existing approaches that rely on read depth or predefined genomic features, this method uses the information content of chromatin accessibility profiles to distinguish high-quality nuclei. This offers a more principled denoising strategy for scATAC-seq analysis.

Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

The authors propose a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, training a gradient-boosted meta-classifier on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe) so that method weighting varies edge by edge rather than being fixed globally. They also develop a diagnostic typology characterizing why methods disagree on individual edges. Evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens, the approach aims to resolve inter-method conflicts more intelligently than picking one method or simple averaging.

TAKO is a new computational framework that ranks the likely impact of single-gene knockouts using only wild-type single-cell RNA-seq data, without needing any knockout-specific training.
