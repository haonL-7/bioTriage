Kintsugi decides, gene by gene, where spatial transcriptomics borrows information.

Kintsugi is a new method for subcellular spatial transcriptomics that, instead of using a fixed smoothing scale or cell outline, uses held-out molecules to decide gene by gene how much to borrow information from spatial neighbors and from other genes at the same location. This suggests that letting the data choose the borrowing structure improves detection of biological signals in sparse spatial data.
