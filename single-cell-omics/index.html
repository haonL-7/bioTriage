Iterative Geometric Structure Guided Nonnegative Matrix Factorization Scheme for Bulk Tissue RNA-seq Data Deconvolution.

This paper introduces an iterative geometric structure-guided NMF scheme for complete deconvolution of bulk RNA-seq data, aiming to jointly estimate cell-type proportions and cell-type-specific gene expression profiles without reference profiles. The method addresses the mathematical ill-posedness of standard NMF by incorporating geometric structure to constrain and improve the solution. It broadens applicability to tissues or species lacking reference data.

Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

The authors propose a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, using a gradient-boosted meta-classifier trained on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe) to decide which method to trust for each regulator-target pair. They also develop a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflicts. Evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens, the approach aims to improve on the common practice of picking one method or averaging all of them.
