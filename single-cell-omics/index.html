Interpretable multi-omics integration across mixed-order tensors with MANTRA.

MANTRA is a probabilistic framework that integrates multi-omics datasets with mixed-order tensors, such as a third-order drug-response tensor alongside a second-order transcriptomics matrix, by combining group factor analysis with tensor decomposition.
