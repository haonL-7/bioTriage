Kintsugi decides, gene by gene, where spatial transcriptomics borrows information.

Kintsugi is a new computational method for subcellular spatial transcriptomics that, instead of using a fixed smoothing scale or cell boundary, uses held-out molecules to decide gene by gene how much to borrow information from spatial neighbors versus other genes at the same location. The work shows that letting the data choose the borrowing structure improves signal recovery in sparse subcellular measurements.
