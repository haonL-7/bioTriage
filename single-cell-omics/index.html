Lossless compression of protein databases for efficient and accurate metagenomic sequence classification with Centrifuger.

Centrifuger introduces a lossless compression algorithm for protein database indexes that shrinks the Ferragina-Manzini (FM) index using a new run-block compression scheme that scales better with alphabet size. Gravlax: an annotation-independent molecular evidence archive for single-cell RNA-seq.

Gravlax introduces an annotation-independent archive that stores molecular evidence from single-cell RNA-seq experiments in a compact form, rather than relying on the gene-by-cell count matrix that depends on a specific gene annotation. This allows the underlying evidence to be reinterpreted as annotations are revised, without needing to reprocess raw reads or alignments. The approach addresses the loss of information and high cost of reanalysis inherent in current single-cell data storage practices.
