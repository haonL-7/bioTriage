Genomic correlates of metastatic competence and progression in human melanoma.

This study sequenced 247 primary cutaneous melanomas and 60 matched metastases to identify genomic features that distinguish tumors that metastasize from those that do not. Relapse was not explained by oncogenic point mutations, which were largely shared between primaries and metastases, but rather by somatic copy-number alterations and global chromosomal instability. The findings suggest that chromosomal-level genomic changes, rather than specific driver mutations, confer metastatic competence in melanoma.
