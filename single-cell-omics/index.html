Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

The authors propose a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, training a gradient-boosted classifier on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe) so that method weighting varies by edge rather than being fixed globally. They also develop a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict. Evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens, the approach aims to resolve disagreements between inference methods more intelligently than selecting one method or simple averaging.

A categorization of spatial transcriptomics methods for cell-cell communication analysis.

This review categorizes 33 recent computational methods for inferring cell-cell communication from spatial transcriptomics data into three groups: cell-type or single-cell resolution network inference, modeling of microenvironment-driven transcriptional variability, and regulatory modulation. It highlights how spatial context enables more biologically grounded communication inference than scRNA-seq alone and maps methods to the distinct biological questions they address.

A contextual activity score (CAS) for inferring ADAR-associated transcriptional activity across RNA-seq, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics.

The authors introduce a Contextual Activity Score (CAS), a new metric for inferring ADAR-associated RNA editing activity from gene expression data rather than raw sequencing reads. Unlike the standard Alu Editing Index, CAS is designed to work across bulk RNA-seq, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics, where read-level editing quantification is impractical. This provides a broadly applicable framework for studying ADAR activity in contexts previously inaccessible to existing methods.

HOMED enables hierarchical and multimodal optimization of DNA methylation deconvolution across tissues.

HOMED is a new computational framework for DNA methylation deconvolution that accounts for hierarchical relationships among related cell types and uses single-cell RNA-seq and paired bulk RNA-seq/DNAm data to optimize CpG signatures. This improves the accuracy of estimating cell-type proportions from bulk methylation data, which is important for epigenome-wide association studies where cellular heterogeneity confounds results. The method was validated across multiple simulated and real datasets.

Categorization and analysis of 16 computational methods for estimating cell potency from single-cell RNA-seq data.

This review categorizes 16 computational methods for estimating cell potency—a cell's differentiation potential—from static single-cell RNA-seq data. The authors group methods into average-based, entropy-based, and knowledge-based approaches based on how they summarize gene expression, and compare them using a unified mathematical framework. The work clarifies the differing assumptions underlying potency inference and provides guidance for choosing appropriate methods.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology 2026-10-23 Medium

Denoising single-cell ATAC-seq data with Shannon entropy.

The authors introduce a Shannon entropy-based method for filtering low-quality and empty droplets in single-cell ATAC-seq data. Unlike existing approaches that rely on read depth or predefined genomic features, this method uses the information content of the chromatin accessibility landscape itself to distinguish high-quality nuclei from noisy droplets. This helps preserve rare cell populations that might otherwise be obscured by debris-derived reads.
