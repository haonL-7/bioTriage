This study sequenced 247 primary cutaneous melanomas and 60 matched metastases to identify genomic events that enable primary tumors to spread. Relapse was not distinguished by oncogenic point mutations, which were largely shared between primaries and metastases, but rather by somatic copy-number alterations and global chromosomal instability. The findings suggest that chromosomal instability, not specific driver mutations, is a key determinant of metastatic competence in melanoma.
