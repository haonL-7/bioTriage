PRISM-GEP: Viewing single-cell expression through the lens of topic modeling.

PRISM-GEP is a new computational method that uses topic modeling to identify gene-expression programs (co-regulated gene modules) from single-cell RNA-seq data without forcing each gene into a single program. The tool offers a more realistic lens for decomposing single-cell expression into overlapping biological processes.
