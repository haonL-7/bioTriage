HIT-MAP: A scalable approach to multimodal mapping of subcellular organization.

HIT-MAP is an end-to-end framework that combines endogenous epitope tagging, optimized wide-field immunofluorescence imaging, and affinity purification-mass spectrometry to map protein subcellular localization and interactions in a coordinated, scalable way. The approach aims to make multimodal subcellular mapping accessible beyond large consortia by reducing per-target costs and eliminating reliance on target-specific antibodies or cDNA libraries. A pilot application to 16 targets demonstrates feasibility of the integrated pipeline.
