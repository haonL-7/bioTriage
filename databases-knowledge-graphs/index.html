iMPI: An Integrative Database for MicroProteins Encoded by Intron Retention in Tumors.

This paper introduces iMPI, an integrative database cataloging microproteins that could be encoded by retained introns in tumors. The authors investigated whether retained introns contain undiscovered protein-coding ORFs and whether these can produce proteins, addressing a gap in resources linking intron retention to cancer biology. The database aims to support research into the role of intron retention-derived microproteins in cancer pathogenesis and therapeutic resistance.

PloS one 2026--01 Medium

Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered Andean whiptail lizard, using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing. The assembly is 1.68 Gb with only 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane lineage.
