A Draft Genome Assembly of Argania spinosa Provides Insights into Lipid Metabolism-Associated Genes and Repeat-Rich Genome Architecture.

This study presents a draft genome assembly of Argania spinosa, the economically important Moroccan argan tree, generated from ~82.7 GB of Illumina short-read data. The assembly is highly fragmented (N50 of 6.84 kb, 139,591 sequences) and incomplete (only 59.1% complete BUSCOs), but the authors use it to identify lipid metabolism-associated genes and characterize the repeat-rich genome architecture. The work provides a first genotype-specific genomic resource for a stress-tolerant oilseed tree, though the assembly quality limits downstream analyses.

This integration aims to improve genomic surveillance and discovery of resistance mechanisms.
