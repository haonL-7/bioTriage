Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered Andean lizard, using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing. The assembly is 1.68 Gb with 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane lineage.
