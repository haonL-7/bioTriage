Defining the Genetic and Phenotypic Landscape of Primary Immune Regulatory Disorders Using the ClinGen Validation Framework.

This preprint applies the ClinGen gene-disease validity framework to primary immune regulatory disorders (PIRDs), a growing subset of inborn errors of immunity marked by autoimmunity, inflammation, and lymphoproliferation. By systematically curating published evidence, the authors aim to define which gene-disease relationships are well-supported versus uncertain, providing a standardized landscape of the genetic and phenotypic features of PIRDs. The work is intended to improve diagnostic interpretation and guide future research in this clinically heterogeneous group of disorders.

Utilization of advanced machine learning based on financial ontology induction for adaptive multilingual method in document recognition.

The paper introduces a noise-robust machine learning framework for inducing financial ontologies across multiple languages from noisy, partially labeled documents. It uses confidence-based filtering, multi-view distillation, and uncertainty-aware contrastive learning to align financial entities across languages. The resulting ontology supports adaptive multilingual document recognition.

Rabies Virus Glycoprotein G Variation Is Associated with Neurovirulence and Reduced Vamp2 Abundance in a Mouse Model of Direct CNS Infection.

This study compared two recombinant rabies viruses differing only in their glycoprotein G, finding that the G protein from the virulent CVS11 strain increased early neuronal infectivity and neurovirulence after direct CNS injection in mice, and was associated with reduced Vamp2 abundance. The results suggest that glycoprotein G variation contributes to rabies neurovirulence partly through effects on synaptic proteins like Vamp2.

Association mapping for canopy temperature under irrigated and rainfed conditions at 12 environments identified both known and novel genetic loci.

This study performed association mapping in 205 diverse soybean lines to identify genetic loci controlling canopy temperature, a proxy for drought tolerance, across 12 irrigated and rainfed environments. The analysis uncovered both previously known and novel genetic loci associated with canopy temperature variation. These findings provide candidate genomic regions for breeding soybean varieties with improved water-use efficiency and drought resilience.

iMPI: An Integrative Database for MicroProteins Encoded by Intron Retention in Tumors.

This paper introduces iMPI, a database cataloging microproteins that could be produced from retained introns in tumor cells. The authors systematically scanned retained introns for protein-coding open reading frames, addressing a gap in resources linking aberrant intron retention to potential tumor-specific proteins. The database aims to support research into how these microproteins may contribute to cancer pathogenesis and therapeutic resistance.

Functional mapping of the Trypanosoma cruzi serinome by fluorophosphonate activity-based protein profiling.

This study maps the active serine hydrolase enzymes (the serinome) of Trypanosoma cruzi, the parasite causing Chagas disease, by combining genome-based prediction with activity-based protein profiling using fluorophosphonate probes. Whole-cell labeling and quantitative proteomics identified 37 enriched serine hydrolase-like proteins, many previously uncharacterized. This provides a functional enzyme inventory that could guide drug target discovery for Chagas disease.

Beyond the autonomous individual: hydrosocial relations and the constitution of the relational hydrosocial self in an Andean conflict.

This qualitative sociological study examines how human subjectivity is shaped through relationships with water systems and territory in an Andean watershed conflict in Peru.
