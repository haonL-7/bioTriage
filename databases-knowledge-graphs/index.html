EchoGO: annotation-context-aware functional interpretation of transcriptomic data.

EchoGO is a reproducible framework for functional enrichment of transcriptomic data that keeps GOseq as the primary evidence while comparing results across multiple organism annotation contexts using experiment-specific backgrounds. It assembles evidence at the GO-term level with analytical and organism provenance, helping address incomplete annotation and uneven identifier recognition. The tool is aimed at making downstream functional interpretation more transparent and reproducible.

InterPro: the protein sequence classification resource in 2027.

InterPro is a freely accessible database that classifies protein sequences by integrating predictive models (signatures) from multiple member databases to assign proteins to families and identify domains and functional sites. The 2027 update provides annotations for hundreds of millions of sequences, achieving extensive coverage of UniProtKB and linking to major resources such as Gene Ontology and PDBe. This makes it a central reference for protein functional annotation.

Cutibacterium acnes induces T-cell extracellular traps with distinct proteome programs.

This study shows that both acne-associated and healthy skin-associated strains of Cutibacterium acnes trigger rapid extracellular trap release from human neutrophils and Th17 T cells. While neutrophil extracellular traps had a conserved proteomic profile regardless of strain, T-cell extracellular traps induced by the two strains displayed distinct proteomic programs, suggesting strain-specific functional consequences in acne pathogenesis.

Integration of genotyping by sequencing and machine learning for genome-wide identification of informative markers for body weight in broiler chickens.

This study combined cost-effective genotyping-by-sequencing (GBS) with two ensemble machine learning methods, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting Machine, to identify SNP markers associated with body weight at hatch and 3 weeks in broiler chickens. The approach demonstrates that GBS plus ML can serve as an affordable alternative to high-density SNP arrays for genome-wide association work in poultry breeding. It identifies informative markers that could support marker-assisted selection for growth traits.

Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

The authors compute phenologs—pairs of human diseases and model-organism phenotypes whose orthologous genes overlap more than expected by chance—at scale using the Monarch Knowledge Graph, which integrates gene-disease, gene-phenotype, and orthology data across human and seven model species. Using PANTHER protein families as an orthology framework, they generate a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases, demonstrating that distant model organisms can yield disease gene candidates.
