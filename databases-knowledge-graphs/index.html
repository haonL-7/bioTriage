Utilization of advanced machine learning based on financial ontology induction for adaptive multilingual method in document recognition.

This paper presents a semi-supervised machine learning framework for inducing financial ontologies across multiple languages from noisy, partially labeled documents. It uses confidence-based filtering, multi-view distillation, and uncertainty-aware contrastive learning to align financial entities across languages despite unreliable data. The result is a more noise-robust method for cross-lingual document recognition in the financial domain.

Rabies Virus Glycoprotein G Variation Is Associated with Neurovirulence and Reduced Vamp2 Abundance in a Mouse Model of Direct CNS Infection.

This study created two recombinant rabies viruses differing only in their glycoprotein G, derived from either an attenuated or a virulent strain. The virulent G variant showed higher early neuronal infectivity and greater neurovirulence after direct brain injection in mice, and was linked to reduced levels of the synaptic protein Vamp2. The findings suggest that glycoprotein G variation contributes to rabies neurovirulence partly by disrupting Vamp2-dependent neuronal function.

Beyond the autonomous individual: hydrosocial relations and the constitution of the relational hydrosocial self in an Andean conflict.

This qualitative sociological study examines how human subjectivity is shaped through relationships with water systems, territory, and nonhuman entities in the Razuhuillca micro-watershed in the Peruvian Andes. Using interviews, walking ethnography, and field observation, it argues against the autonomous, bounded, human-centered self and proposes a 'relational hydrosocial self' constituted through ecological relations. The work is a theoretical and ethnographic contribution to social theory rather than a data-driven or computational study.
