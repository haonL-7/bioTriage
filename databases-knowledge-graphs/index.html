Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

This preprint scales up the phenolog approach—identifying non-obvious gene-phenotype connections between humans and model organisms—by computing them across the Monarch Knowledge Graph, which integrates gene-disease, gene-phenotype, and orthology data for humans and seven model species. Using PANTHER protein families as orthology groupings, the authors generate a catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases, including cross-species links such as mouse-to-slime-mold. The work provides a broad, reusable resource for prioritizing disease-gene candidates from model-organism annotations.
