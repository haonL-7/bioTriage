Multi-omics-constrained kinetic models quantify pathway rewiring and predict metabolic vulnerabilities in BRCA1-deficient ovarian cancer.

The authors built near-genome-scale kinetic models of metabolism constrained by multi-omics data for isogenic BRCA1 wild-type and BRCA1-mutant ovarian cancer cells, covering over 2,100 reactions. Using these ensembles, they performed uncertainty-quantified perturbation simulations and metabolic control analysis, identifying 28 enzyme-mediated metabolic vulnerabilities, most of which map to existing or approved drugs. Independent DepMap data supported the predictions, suggesting the approach can reveal pathway rewiring and druggable liabilities in BRCA1-deficient tumors.
