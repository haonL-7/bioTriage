iMPI: An Integrative Database for MicroProteins Encoded by Intron Retention in Tumors.

This paper introduces iMPI, an integrative database cataloging microproteins that could be encoded by retained introns in tumors. The authors systematically investigated whether retained introns contain undiscovered protein-coding ORFs and whether these can produce proteins, addressing a gap in tumor intron resources. The database aims to support research into the role of aberrant intron retention in cancer pathogenesis and therapeutic resistance.

Functional mapping of the Trypanosoma cruzi serinome by fluorophosphonate activity-based protein profiling.

This study maps the active serine hydrolase enzymes (the 'serinome') of Trypanosoma cruzi, the parasite that causes Chagas disease, using fluorophosphonate-based activity-based protein profiling combined with quantitative proteomics. The authors identified 37 enriched serine hydrolase-like proteins in the epimastigote stage, providing a functional annotation resource for this largely uncharacterized enzyme family. This chemoproteomic map could guide future drug target discovery against Chagas disease.

PloS one 2026--01 Medium

Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered Andean lizard, using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing. The assembly is 1.68 Gb with 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane lineage.
