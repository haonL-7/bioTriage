Lessons From CABBI: Advancing Vegetative Oil Production in C4 Crops Through a Vertically Integrated DBTL Framework.

This paper describes how the CABBI research center organized a vertically integrated Design-Build-Test-Learn framework to engineer C4 grasses (including sugarcane and sorghum) to accumulate triacylglycerol oil in vegetative biomass rather than just seeds. Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

The authors computed phenologs—statistical overlaps between human disease genes and model-organism phenotype genes linked by orthology—at knowledge-graph scale using the Monarch Knowledge Graph, covering human and seven model species. Using PANTHER protein families as orthology units, they produced a catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases, including cross-species links such as mouse-to-slime-mold. This provides a broad, principled resource for prioritizing disease-gene candidates from model-organism annotations.

PDBe-SIFTS is a new open-source, locally deployable tool that maps UniProt protein sequences to Protein Data Bank structures at residue-level resolution. It improves on the original SIFTS pipeline by using MMseqs2 for fast sequence search, a better scoring scheme for ranking candidate mappings, and refinement based on backbone connectivity. This makes high-quality structure-function mapping accessible to any lab without relying on internal PDBe infrastructure.
