Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

The authors compute 'phenologs'—pairs of human diseases and model-organism phenotypes whose orthologous genes overlap more than expected by chance—at knowledge-graph scale using the Monarch Knowledge Graph, which integrates gene-disease, gene-phenotype, and orthology data across human and seven model species. Cutibacterium acnes induces T-cell extracellular traps with distinct proteome programs.

This study shows that both acne-associated and healthy skin-associated strains of Cutibacterium acnes trigger rapid extracellular trap release from human neutrophils and Th17 cells. While neutrophil extracellular traps had a conserved proteomic profile regardless of strain, T-cell extracellular traps (TETs) exhibited distinct proteome programs depending on the C. acnes strain. This suggests that TETs may contribute to acne pathogenesis in a strain-specific manner.
