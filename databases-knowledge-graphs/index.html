PDBe-KB Complexes: Enabling Functional Insight from Macromolecular Complexes in the PDB.

PDBe-KB Complexes is a new resource that groups equivalent experimentally determined macromolecular assemblies from the PDB into unified complex-level records, consolidating data on component identity, stoichiometry, symmetry, ligands, and PISA-derived properties. This provides a consistent framework for gaining functional insight from macromolecular complexes.

Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered Andean lizard, using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing. The assembly is 1.68 Gb with 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will aid conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane lineage.
