Defining the Genetic and Phenotypic Landscape of Primary Immune Regulatory Disorders Using the ClinGen Validation Framework.

This preprint applies the ClinGen gene-disease validity framework to primary immune regulatory disorders (PIRDs), a growing subset of inborn errors of immunity marked by autoimmunity, inflammation, and lymphoproliferation. Rabies Virus Glycoprotein G Variation Is Associated with Neurovirulence and Reduced Vamp2 Abundance in a Mouse Model of Direct CNS Infection.

Researchers created two recombinant rabies viruses that differ only in their glycoprotein G, derived from either an attenuated or a virulent strain. The virulent G variant showed higher early neuronal infectivity and greater neurovirulence after direct brain injection in mice, and was linked to reduced levels of the synaptic protein Vamp2, suggesting G contributes to neuronal dysfunction.
