HIT-MAP: A scalable approach to multimodal mapping of subcellular organization.

HIT-MAP is an end-to-end framework that combines endogenous epitope tagging, optimized wide-field immunofluorescence imaging, and affinity purification-mass spectrometry to map subcellular protein organization in a coordinated, scalable way. By reducing reliance on target-specific antibodies or cDNA libraries and providing unified data acquisition pipelines, it aims to make multimodal subcellular mapping accessible beyond large consortia. The pilot application to 16 targets demonstrates feasibility of the integrated workflow.

This study applies the ClinGen gene-disease validity framework to systematically classify the strength of evidence linking genes to primary immune regulatory disorders (PIRDs), a growing subset of inborn errors of immunity. This provides a curated resource to guide clinical genetic testing and diagnosis for these complex immune conditions.

PDBe-KB Complexes: Enabling Functional Insight from Macromolecular Complexes in the PDB.

PDBe-KB Complexes is a new resource that groups equivalent macromolecular assemblies from the PDB into unified complex-level records, consolidating data on component identity, stoichiometry, symmetry, ligands, and PISA-derived properties. It also captures relationships between complexes, such as subcomplexes, providing a consistent framework for functional interpretation of protein assemblies.
