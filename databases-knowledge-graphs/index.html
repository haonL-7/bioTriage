Rabies Virus Glycoprotein G Variation Is Associated with Neurovirulence and Reduced Vamp2 Abundance in a Mouse Model of Direct CNS Infection.

This study compared two recombinant rabies viruses that differ only in their glycoprotein G, one from an attenuated vaccine strain and one from a virulent strain. The virulent G variant infected neurons more efficiently and caused more severe neurological disease after direct brain injection in mice, and this was linked to reduced levels of the synaptic protein Vamp2. The findings suggest that glycoprotein G variation contributes to rabies neurovirulence partly by disrupting Vamp2-dependent neuronal function.

iMPI: An Integrative Database for MicroProteins Encoded by Intron Retention in Tumors.

The authors built iMPI, an integrative database cataloging microproteins that could be produced from retained introns in tumor transcriptomes. By systematically scanning retained introns for protein-coding ORFs, they provide a resource linking aberrant intron retention to potential tumor-specific microproteins. This fills a gap in tumor-focused databases and may support cancer biomarker and therapeutic target discovery.
