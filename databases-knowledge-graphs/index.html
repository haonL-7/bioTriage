Multi-omics-constrained kinetic models quantify pathway rewiring and predict metabolic vulnerabilities in BRCA1-deficient ovarian cancer.

The authors built near-genome-scale kinetic models of metabolism (over 2,100 reactions) constrained by multi-omics data for isogenic BRCA1 wild-type and BRCA1-mutant ovarian cancer cells.
