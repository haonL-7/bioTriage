HIT-MAP: A scalable approach to multimodal mapping of subcellular organization.

HIT-MAP is an end-to-end framework that combines endogenous epitope tagging, optimized wide-field immunofluorescence imaging, and affinity purification-mass spectrometry to map protein subcellular localization and interactions in a coordinated, scalable way. It lowers the cost and technical barriers that previously restricted multimodal subcellular mapping to large consortia, demonstrated in a pilot of 16 targets.
