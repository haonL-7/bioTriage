Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

This paper computes 'phenologs'—statistically significant overlaps between human disease genes and model-organism phenotype genes linked by orthology—at scale using the Monarch Knowledge Graph, covering human and seven model species including mouse and slime mold. The result is a catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases, demonstrating that even distantly related organisms can nominate disease-gene candidates.
