This paper describes how the CABBI center used a vertically integrated Design-Build-Test-Learn framework to engineer C4 grasses (such as sugarcane, sorghum, and miscanthus) to accumulate triacylglycerol (oil) in vegetative tissues for renewable fuels and biobased chemicals. Phenologs at knowledge-graph scale: a mouse-to-slime-mold catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases.

The authors compute 'phenologs'—pairs of human diseases and model-organism phenotypes whose associated genes overlap more than expected by chance after orthology mapping—at knowledge-graph scale using the Monarch Knowledge Graph, covering human and seven model species. Using PANTHER protein families as an orthology framework, they produce a catalog of candidate genes for thousands of human diseases, including cross-species links such as mouse-to-slime-mold. This provides a systematic, principled resource for transferring gene-phenotype knowledge from model organisms to human disease.

PDBe-SIFTS is a new open-source, locally deployable tool that maps residues between UniProt sequences and Protein Data Bank structures, replacing the previous internal-only pipeline. It uses MMseqs2 for fast sequence search, a new bounded scoring scheme for ranking candidate mappings, and backbone-connectivity-based refinement of residue-level mappings. This makes high-quality structure-sequence integration accessible to any lab or resource that needs it.
