HIT-MAP: A scalable approach to multimodal mapping of subcellular organization.

HIT-MAP is an end-to-end framework that combines endogenous epitope tagging, optimized wide-field immunofluorescence imaging, and affinity purification-mass spectrometry to map protein subcellular localization and interactions in a coordinated, scalable way. By reducing reliance on target-specific antibodies or cDNA libraries and providing a unified data acquisition pipeline, it aims to make multimodal subcellular mapping accessible beyond large consortia. The approach was piloted on 16 targets.

This protocol paper proposes developing an ontology for workplace violence (WPV) to enable consistent, cross-sector incident reporting in public services. The authors argue that current sector-specific and organization-specific reporting categories, combined with poor incident descriptions and no shared nomenclature, cause underreporting and make events incomparable or invisible. The ontology aims to provide a common semantic framework so similar events are recorded consistently across settings.
