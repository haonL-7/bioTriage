Association mapping for canopy temperature under irrigated and rainfed conditions at 12 environments identified both known and novel genetic loci.

This study performed association mapping for canopy temperature in 205 diverse soybean genotypes across 12 irrigated and rainfed environments, identifying both previously known and novel genetic loci linked to drought response. The authors developed GENIAL, a framework combining gene regulatory network (GRN) refinement with topological data analysis, and TomTom, a knowledge graph integrating 11 public databases, to study how tomato plants coordinate responses to multiple pathogens simultaneously. iMPI: An Integrative Database for MicroProteins Encoded by Intron Retention in Tumors.

This paper introduces iMPI, a database cataloging microproteins that could be produced from retained introns in tumor cells. The authors systematically scanned retained introns across cancers for protein-coding open reading frames, addressing a gap in resources linking intron retention to potential tumor-specific proteins. The database aims to support research into how these cryptic microproteins contribute to cancer biology and therapeutic resistance.

Bioorganic & medicinal chemistry 2026-Aug-29 Medium

Functional mapping of the Trypanosoma cruzi serinome by fluorophosphonate activity-based protein profiling.

This study maps the active serine hydrolases (the 'serinome') of Trypanosoma cruzi, the parasite causing Chagas disease, using cell-permeable fluorophosphonate activity-based probes combined with quantitative proteomics. The authors identified 37 enriched serine hydrolase-like proteins, providing a functional annotation of this large but poorly characterized enzyme family in the parasite. This chemoproteomic resource could help identify new drug targets for Chagas disease.

Piecewise latent growth modeling as the methodological standard for evaluation research.

This paper argues that evaluation research should move beyond simple pre-post difference scores, which treat change as a discrete event, and instead adopt piecewise latent growth modeling (PLGM) to capture change as a continuous, phase-structured process. The authors provide a theoretical and methodological case for PLGM as the standard across clinical, educational, and organizational evaluation contexts.

PloS one 2026--01 Medium

Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered lizard endemic to a small region in southern Ecuador. Using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing, the authors produced a highly contiguous 1.68 Gb assembly with 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane species.
