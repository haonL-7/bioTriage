Multimodality, interaction modeling, and multimodule architectures in genomic prediction: A unified conceptual framework.

This paper proposes a unified conceptual framework to clarify three frequently conflated terms in genomic prediction: multimodality (integrating diverse data types), interaction modeling (capturing non-additive effects like epistasis or GxE), and multimodule architectures (computational designs with separate processing branches). Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a Critically Endangered lizard endemic to a narrow region in southern Ecuador. Using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing, the authors produced a 1.68 Gb assembly with only 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this montane lineage.
