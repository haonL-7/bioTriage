Defining the Genetic and Phenotypic Landscape of Primary Immune Regulatory Disorders Using the ClinGen Validation Framework.

This study applies the ClinGen gene-disease validity framework to systematically classify the strength of evidence linking genes to primary immune regulatory disorders (PIRDs), a growing subset of inborn errors of immunity. By standardizing how genetic and phenotypic evidence is evaluated, the work clarifies which gene-disease relationships are well-supported versus uncertain, helping resolve inconsistencies in the published literature. The result is a curated framework that can guide clinical genetic testing and diagnosis for these immune disorders.
