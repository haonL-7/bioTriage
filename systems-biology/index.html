Let there be multifunctionality: Uncovering the dynamical zoo of the AC-DC genetic circuit.

This paper analyzes the AC-DC genetic circuit, a minimal three-gene network combining a toggle switch and a repressilator, and shows that a single-inducer version can simultaneously exhibit oscillations and multistability. Using thermodynamic modeling and Bayesian inference, the authors map a diverse 'zoo' of dynamical behaviors, classify them, and assess robustness. The work challenges the traditional separation of oscillatory and multistable dynamics in gene regulatory networks.
