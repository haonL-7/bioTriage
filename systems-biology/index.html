Protocol for thermodynamic analysis of genome-scale metabolic models using dGbyG and ThermoInfer.

This protocol describes a computational workflow combining two tools, dGbyG and ThermoInfer, to add thermodynamic constraints to genome-scale metabolic models. dGbyG predicts standard Gibbs free energy changes for reactions, and ThermoInfer then uses these to perform thermodynamic flux balance analysis and flag thermodynamically inconsistent reactions. The authors demonstrate the workflow on the Yeast9-GEM model.
