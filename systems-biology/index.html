Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

This paper introduces a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, using a gradient-boosted meta-classifier trained on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe). Rather than choosing one method or averaging all, the classifier learns edge-specific weights, and the authors also provide a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflicts. The work is evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens.

Exact Discrete Stochastic Simulation With Deep-Learning-Scale Gradient Optimization.

The authors present a method that makes exact Gillespie-type stochastic simulations of continuous-time Markov chains differentiable, enabling gradient-based optimization at scales far beyond previous approaches. They achieve this by decoupling forward simulation (hard categorical sampling for exact trajectories) from backward differentiation (a massively parallel Gumbel-Softmax straight-through surrogate). This allows deep-learning-scale optimization of stochastic biochemical and other discrete systems while preserving exact simulation fidelity.
