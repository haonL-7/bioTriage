Let there be multifunctionality: Uncovering the dynamical zoo of the AC-DC genetic circuit.

This study analyzes the AC-DC genetic circuit, a three-gene network combining a toggle switch and a repressilator, and shows that a single-inducer version can exhibit multiple dynamical behaviors simultaneously, including coexisting oscillations and multistability.
