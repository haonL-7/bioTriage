This protocol describes a computational workflow that combines two algorithms, dGbyG and ThermoInfer, to add thermodynamic constraints to genome-scale metabolic models. dGbyG predicts standard Gibbs free energy changes for reactions, and ThermoInfer then performs thermodynamic flux balance analysis to flag reactions that are thermodynamically inconsistent.
