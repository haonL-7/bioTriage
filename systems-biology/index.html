Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

This paper introduces a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, using a gradient-boosted meta-classifier trained on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe).
