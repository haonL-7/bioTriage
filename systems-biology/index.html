CardamomOT: A mechanistic optimal transport-based framework for gene regulatory network inference, trajectory reconstruction and generative modeling.

CardamomOT extends the CARDAMOM algorithm for inferring gene regulatory networks from single-cell RNA-seq time courses by incorporating optimal transport, which allows the method to use actual time-point information rather than just relative ordering of snapshots.
