The paper proposes a conceptual framework explaining why interventions that extend lifespan in simple model organisms (e.g., yeast, worms, flies) have diminishing effects in mammals. The authors integrate comparative experimental data with mechanistic insights to argue this transition sets a biological ceiling on lifespan extension.
