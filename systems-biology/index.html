Protocol for thermodynamic analysis of genome-scale metabolic models using dGbyG and ThermoInfer.

This protocol describes how to combine two computational tools, dGbyG and ThermoInfer, to add thermodynamic constraints to genome-scale metabolic models. dGbyG predicts standard Gibbs free energy changes for reactions, and ThermoInfer uses these to perform thermodynamic flux balance analysis and flag thermodynamically inconsistent reactions. The workflow is demonstrated on the Yeast9-GEM model.
