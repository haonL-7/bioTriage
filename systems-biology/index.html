Disagreement-informed arbitration for gene regulatory network inference: A score-level meta-classifier and a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflict.

The authors propose a per-edge arbitration approach for gene regulatory network inference, training a gradient-boosted classifier on the raw scores from ten different inference methods (including GENIE3, GRNBoost2, CLR, and ARACNe) so that method weighting varies by edge rather than being fixed globally. They also develop a diagnostic typology of inter-method conflicts. Evaluated across six single-cell perturbation screens, the approach aims to resolve disagreements between inference methods more effectively than selecting one method or simple averaging.
